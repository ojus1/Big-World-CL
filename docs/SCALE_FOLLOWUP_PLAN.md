# Full comparison after scale-v1 hardening

This is an implementation and launch plan, **not a preregistration or permission
to modify scale-v1**. All six original slots remain in its evidence inventory;
any still-running worlds continue within their original limits. Interruptions
remain documented in the [execution observations](SCALE_EXECUTION_NOTES.md) and
[later learning interruption](SCALE_ADOPTION_INTERRUPTION.md). The active objective remains a
full multi-enterprise, multi-employee, three-seed comparison with repeated
employee learning and prospective work after learning.

## Preserve the comparison's scope

A follow-up development campaign will launch six fresh worlds: both no-learning
Hermes and pinned SkillOpt for each of three seeds. Each world retains four
enterprises, twelve employees, eight consumers, one government agency, twenty
action days and two settlement-only days. Fixed demand remains 240 commitments
per world. Institutions continue to react to competitors, policy, geopolitics and
work outcomes. All three workflows and all four requirement regimes remain.

The complete seed/cohort set from scale-v1 can be reused for operational
development. Reuse must include all three pairs, with a new source/configuration
manifest and new actor environments. Surviving old arms cannot be combined with
rerun arms to assemble the new comparison. An independent confirmation later
needs newly held-out scenarios and cohorts; this development rerun must not be
presented as one.

Retain the declared SkillOpt baseline and its gate. A change to TRAIN/VAL counts,
contrastive rollouts, update days, baseline skills or workloads requires a
separately declared experimental design. Neither positive learning gain nor a
minimum adoption count is a launch or stopping criterion.

## Required engineering and preflight evidence

| Requirement | Evidence required before registration |
| --- | --- |
| Consistent learning-stop accounting | Reviewed V2 raw, gate, transfer and scale audits; known costs preserved; incomplete updates cannot adopt. |
| Canonical CLI provenance | Actual module entrypoint resolves the native actor/executor; historical evidence remains auditable at its matching revision. |
| Structured actor output | Reviewed opt-in API/IPC/native/bridge/client path; schemas, actor identity, logical request, cache and trace bindings; no hidden retries; usage and deadline checks. |
| Work transport reliability | Reviewed opt-in non-streaming compatibility adapter if adopted; native request/response, tool, reasoning, status and cancellation parity. |
| Native capability | Separately recorded, bounded live preflight of the selected actor and employee transports, using synthetic fixtures outside the study. |
| Horizon feasibility | Duration and receipt observations across workflows, regimes, no-op/proposal learning epochs and planned concurrency. |
| Publication | All planned slots retained, unknown costs distinct from measurements, no interrupted-world zero filling, raw artifacts kept private. |

The actor contract and employee transport option must be identical across paired
arms. Any opt-in change must be forwarded to online work and every relevant
learning replay, recorded in provenance, and checked by the auditor. A patched
server alone or an accepted configuration flag is not proof that the native
execution used the requested behavior.

Native preflight comes after offline compatibility tests and independent review.
It uses a fresh isolated installation or waits until the current stack can be
changed safely; it must never install code into the active scale-v1 services.
Record its source/configuration hashes, physical dispatches, receipts, failures
and bounds before evaluating whether the stack is ready. A successful mock or
single request does not establish long-horizon reliability.

## Budgets and failure policy

The current ten-hour world ceiling is a stopping bound, not a guarantee of
twenty-day completion. Individual maxima permit 240 online attempts at 420
seconds each, or 28 hours of online work alone; 36 learning epochs at 1,800 seconds
permit another 18 hours. Actor/bootstrap work adds time. These are arithmetic
maxima, not duration predictions, and two early epochs cannot forecast the whole
campaign. Choose and publish the follow-up's aggregate limits using preflight
evidence before launching its study worlds. Keep corresponding limits symmetric
between arms and disclose additional learning compute.

Non-streaming may reduce the observed SSE failure mode, but it cannot eliminate
network uncertainty. The evidence-preservation change does not recover lost
usage. Keep unknown usage invalid under the current reviewed accounting contract;
any alternative policy needs separate prospective justification and review.
Preserve request intent, known costs and uncertain reservations after failures.
Do not replay uncertain actions or reopen reconstructed actor state as though it
were the original environment.

## Launch and completion gates

After the prerequisite evidence passes, prepare a new complete campaign,
independently review its exact configuration and budgets, publish its safe
manifest, and require that manifest's raw hash at execution. Supervise all six
worlds without outcome-conditioned replacements or extensions. Commit reviewed
implementation and diagnostic progress while retaining private trajectories.

The full endpoint requires complete, valid evidence for all three planned pairs,
including correct chronological skill-version exposure and any adoption
decisions, prospective future work, raw grades,
commitment closure, accounting and environment cleanup. Partial worlds remain
useful development evidence, but cannot substitute for that completion gate.
